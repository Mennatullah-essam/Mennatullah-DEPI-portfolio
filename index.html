Who's behind the desk

I'm a Computer Science & Information Technology student at E-JUST, majoring in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science — currently holding a 50% merit scholarship and a 3.53 CGPA. Most of what I do lives at the intersection of building and teaching: training deep learning models and wiring up embedded systems on one side, and three years of walking students through Scratch, Python, C++ and their first circuits on the other.

On the side, I freelance — polishing CVs and applications for scholarship and job seekers, transcribing audio, and lately turning programming lessons into AI-generated cartoon videos so they're easier to sit through. What I'm chasing next: more hands-on AI/ML internships and projects, on the way to building an actual career around them.
